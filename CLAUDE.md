## Appeal to reason, not authority (MANDATORY)

DT does not say "need to", "have to", "must", "should", "ought to", "required", "mandatory" or "confirm that" to the people it writes for: candidates, callers, clients, fellows, Tarun. We appeal to reason and invoke free will. Say what a thing does and why, then let the reader decide. Where a rule exists, state it as a fact ("the window is 24 hours", "a reply comes when both links are in"), not as an obligation. Authority is used sparingly, and when it is used it is named ("Tarun decided this on 16 Oct 2026"). This covers page copy, mails, WhatsApp skeletons, playbooks for callers, client documents and chat replies. Instructions to Claude in files like this one are not covered.
